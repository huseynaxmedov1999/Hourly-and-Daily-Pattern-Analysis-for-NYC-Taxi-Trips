# _Insights from Hourly and Daily Pattern Analysis for NYC Taxi_

- Dataset consists of approximately 1.46 million taxi trips and 11 columns, providing a large-scale view of NYC transportation behavior.
- The average trip duration is around 16 minutes during peak afternoon hours, with the highest average recorded at 3 PM (15:00) — 16.16 minutes.
- The busiest taxi hour in NYC is 6 PM (18:00) with 90,022 trips, followed closely by 7 PM (19:00) with 89,753 trips, showing strong evening rush-hour activity.
- Early morning hours have the lowest taxi demand, where 5 AM recorded only 14,767 trips, indicating significantly lower transportation activity overnight.
- Friday is the busiest day of the week with 221,870 trips, while Monday has the lowest activity with 186,051 trips, showing increased mobility closer to weekends.
- Vendor 2 handled slightly longer average trips (14.08 minutes) compared to Vendor 1 (13.93 minutes), although the difference between vendors is relatively small.
- Outlier analysis identified extremely abnormal trips, including rides lasting over 1,400 minutes, confirming the necessity of outlier filtering before conducting transportation analysis.
